## Fixes

- **Recovery after a Telegram outage** — after Telegram comes back, the bot answers the next message without a restart and tells you if the last assistant reply was lost or some of your messages were skipped.
- **Scheduled-task schedule parsing** — `/task` now parses the schedule you type with the model you selected for that task. ([@bcncuteapps](https://github.com/bcncuteapps))
